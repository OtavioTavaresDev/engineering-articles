Detection Engineering Is Becoming One of the Most Valuable Security Skills

Why modern security teams increasingly prioritize signal quality over alert volume.

May 14, 2026 9 min read

From Alert Consumer to Detection Creator

Five years ago, SOC analysts spent most of their time triaging alerts from vendors. Today, the most effective teams write their own detections. They understand their environment, their data sources, and their risk profile. Off-the-shelf rules are a starting point, not an ending point.

Detection engineering treats detection logic as code: versioned, tested, reviewed, and continuously improved.

Why It's So Valuable in 2026

  • 📈 Alert fatigue is worse than ever: Companies drown in false positives. Detection engineers reduce noise.
  • 🎯 Attackers are stealthier: Signature-based detections miss living-off-the-land techniques. Behavioral detections require custom engineering.
  • ☁️ Cloud and SaaS generate unique logs: Each environment needs tailored rules for Okta, O365, AWS CloudTrail, etc.
  • 🤖 AI-generated attacks: Offensive AI requires defensive AI — and detection engineers bridge that gap.

Core Skills of a Detection Engineer

  • • Sigma, KQL, SPL, or Lucene
  • • Understanding of attack techniques (MITRE ATT&CK)
  • • Log parsing and normalization
  • • Data analysis with Python/Pandas
  • • Version control for detection rules
  • • Testing and validation frameworks
