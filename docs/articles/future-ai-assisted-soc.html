The Future of AI-Assisted SOC Operations

LLMs, summarization, investigation, and the role of human analysts.

May 25, 2026 11 min read

AI won't replace SOC analysts — but analysts who use AI will replace those who don't. LLMs can triage alerts, suggest investigation steps, and draft incident reports. The future SOC is a human-AI partnership where analysts focus on complex reasoning while AI handles volume.
