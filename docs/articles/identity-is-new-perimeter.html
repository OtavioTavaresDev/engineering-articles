Identity Is the New Perimeter

Why controlling who (and what) can access your systems now matters more than network firewalls.

May 29, 2026 13 min read

The VPN Era Is Over

Traditional security assumed that inside the corporate network was safe. That assumption died with cloud, SaaS, and remote work. Today, your users, data, and applications live everywhere. The only constant is identity — the user, service account, or device making a request.

Attackers know this. They don't break into networks; they steal credentials. In 2025, over 80% of breaches involved compromised identities, according to the Verizon DBIR. MFA helps, but modern attacks bypass it via session hijacking and AiTM phishing.

Core Identity Attack Vectors

  • 🎣 Phishing & AiTM – Steal session tokens after MFA.
  • 🧾 Infostealers – Malware that exfiltrates browser cookies and saved passwords.
  • 🔄 Credential stuffing – Reused passwords from past breaches.
  • 🤖 Service account abuse – Overprivileged non-human identities.

Modern Identity Controls

✅ Recommended

  • • Phishing-resistant MFA (FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys)
  • • Conditional access policies (geo-location, device compliance)
  • • Just-in-time (JIT) access for privileged roles
  • • Continuous access evaluation (CAE) to invalidate sessions
  • • Identity threat detection (UEBA for identity)

Detection Signals for Identity Compromise

  • ✈️ Impossible travel – Login from NY and 5 min later from London.
  • 🔄 Multiple MFA failures then success – Fatigue attack pattern.
  • 🖥️ Unusual user-agent or device – Session replay from different OS.
  • 📎 OAuth app consent to high-risk scopes – Malicious third-party app.
