MDR vs Traditional SOC: Why Managed Detection Is Growing

The shift from in-house alert triage to outcome-driven detection services.

May 18, 2026 10 min read

The SOC Staffing Crisis

Building and running a 24/7 SOC is expensive. Skilled analysts are hard to find, burnout is high, and alert volumes keep growing. As a result, many organizations are turning to Managed Detection and Response (MDR) providers — not just for cost savings, but for detection quality.

MDR providers handle the entire detection pipeline: telemetry ingestion, alert triage, threat hunting, and incident response. The customer gets outcomes, not infrastructure.

Traditional SOC vs. MDR

🏢 Traditional SOC

  • • In-house team, 24/7 shifts
  • • Customer owns SIEM/licensing
  • • Detection rules built internally
  • • High fixed cost

⚡ MDR

  • • Outsourced detection team
  • • Provider manages infrastructure
  • • Shared threat intelligence across customers
  • • Operational expense model

Why MDR Is Winning

  • 📊 Crowdsourced telemetry: MDR sees attacks across thousands of environments, tuning detections faster.
  • 🎯 Focus on response, not just alerting: Most MDRs contain threats, not just notify.
  • 💸 Predictable costs: No surprise SIEM ingestion bills.
