Why SIEM Alone Is No Longer Enough

The shift toward XDR, EDR, and detection engineering over log aggregation.

May 21, 2026 11 min read

The SIEM Promise vs. Reality

SIEMs were designed to centralize logs and enable correlation. In practice, they've become expensive data lakes that generate too many alerts. Organizations spend millions on ingestion and storage, yet their MTTD remains high.

XDR and EDR as the New Core

Extended Detection and Response (XDR) integrates endpoint, network, and cloud telemetry with built-in detections. Unlike SIEM, XDR is optimized for high-fidelity signals, not raw logs. Many SOCs now use SIEM only for compliance and long-term retention, while XDR handles real-time detection.
